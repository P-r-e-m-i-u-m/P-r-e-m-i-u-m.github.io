I'm an independent developer focused on generative AI, Python engineering, and open-source software. Most of my learning happens by building, testing, breaking things, reading the source, and trying again.

My work ranges from OpenAI-compatible infrastructure and AI-assisted developer tools to projects that make contributing to open source less intimidating. I enjoy the space where useful ideas meet careful implementation.

I don't think good engineering is about adding complexity. It's about making the next step clearer, safer, and easier for someone else.
